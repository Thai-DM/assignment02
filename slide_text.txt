Key Takeaways
1 Data comes in many forms: tabular, image, text, audio, video, time series, and graphs.
2 A file format is a storage mechanism, not a learning representation.
3 Numerical data can be organized as scalars, vectors, matrices, and tensors.
4 Different modalities produce different tensor shapes.
5 Shape alone is insufficient: semantics, dtype, range, ordering, and preprocessing also
matter.
6 Correct representation is essential for correct model training and inference.
7 Data representation must be reproducible from training through deployment.
Good Data + Good Representation + Good Evaluation ΓåÆReliable Intelligent System
